AN ADVENTURE THROUGH THE LOST WOODS

THE LEGEND OF THE LABYRINTH

Princess Zelda is trapped deep within the ancient labyrinth. As Link, seek the legendary Master Sword, still sealed in the village altar, and carry your shield and bow until you earn it. Enter the Ember Rift portal to face your own shadow, or collect three sacred seals and enter the separate Shadow Keep, solve its three magical locks, and defeat Ganon inside to rescue the princess. Discover three shrines with different randomly selected puzzles on each playthrough. Blocks, rotating mirrors, rune sequences and linked crystal switches test your ingenuity. Perfectly time shield parries and chain three sword strikes. The ancient Great Tree presides over a protected sanctuary with ten differently shaped elf houses and gardens, sixty named family members, individual layouts, one-time house perks and shops. Wolves and eight-legged Guardians stalk the quieter outer maze. Some innocent-looking travellers are actually banana-obsessed Yiga ninjas: speaking to them reveals their disguise and starts a fight. Each elf home has a unique garden, architecture and once-only household perk. Earn leaf tokens from monsters, speak to the residents, and claim the Master Sword at its altar only after all shrines are cleared. Four mossy arches also link opposite corners of the maze.
